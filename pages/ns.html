Frequently Repeated Concepts & Question Trends

  • 2025 DSSSB Shifts Trend: Direct testing of 2's complement (4-bit 1100 in Shift 1; 8-bit -8 = 11111000 in 13 Sep Shift 3), Hex-to-Decimal (A5_16 = 165 in Shift 3), OR/NOT/XOR gate properties (Shifts 2 & 3), universal gate statements, and 2-level NAND realization of AB + BC (13 Sep Shift 3).
  • 2023 & 2021 DSSSB Shifts Trend: Arithmetic right shift for signed division by 2 (10100 → 11010), C integer prefixes (62, 062, 0x62), base bounds (base-7 max 4-digit is 6666_7), 3-variable K-map wrap-around (∑m(0,2,5,7) = A'C' + AC), NOR-to-AND gate count (3 gates), MUX select formula (n = 2^m), XOR adder-subtractor control, and 2-bit ripple counter tracing.

Limitations in PYQ Verification & Authenticity Policy

  • Where official CBT response sheets record exact test date and shift (e.g., 12 Sep 2025 Shifts 1/2/3, 13 Sep 2025 Shift 3, 25 Jun 2023 Shifts 1/2/3, 1 Aug 2021 Shifts 1/3, 7 Aug 2021 Shifts 2/3, 11 Jul 2021 PGT), the exact shift and date are tagged under Verified PYQ.
  • Where published PYQ repositories verify the examination and year (e.g., DSSSB 2022, DSSSB 2018, KVS 2018, Bihar STET 2025) without shift numbers, the shift is explicitly labeled Shift not verified.
  • Where questions are adapted from KVS/NVS/DSSSB memory-based or topic-wise compilations without an individually verifiable year/shift, they are strictly labeled Adapted PYQ | Year not verified | Shift not verified. Original practice questions are labeled Original Practice and never misrepresented as PYQs.
